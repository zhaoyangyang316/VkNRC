Originally converted from Blender to Mitsuba/Tungsten/PBRT by Benedikt Bitterli.
Converted to OBJ by Nicholas Hull at NVIDIA; material corrections by Morgan
McGuire at Williams College to match Bitterli's reference images.
Camera metadata comes from Bitterli's matching living-room-2 scene:
https://benedikt-bitterli.me/resources/mitsuba/living-room-2.zip

VkNRC adaptations: added a normalized camera preset, converted texture path
separators to '/', and added explicit Beckmann roughness values using
Pr = sqrt(2 / (Ns + 2)). Geometry, textures, light placement and Ke radiance
are retained from the McGuire archive. See README.md for renderer limitations.
